🛡️ The Nova Privacy Promise

How we keep student data safe (COPPA & FERPA aligned).

1. Local-first, always. Your notes, calendar, XP and chat history are stored only in your own browser's local storage. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

2. No data logging. We do not log, store, or record your keystrokes, notes, searches, or chat messages on any backend.

3. No AI retraining on student data. The quiz generator, slideshow maker and tutor run entirely on-device. Your inputs are never used to train or improve any model.

4. No ads, paywalls, or trackers. Nova Workspace is free with no commercial subscription and no advertising network.

5. Minimal, explainable sign-in. Google Sign-in is optional and used only to personalize your profile; no academic data is shared with third parties.

This tool is designed for schools and students. We do not collect personal information beyond what is required to display your own profile.
